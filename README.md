## English

Download **`PSP Flix.exe`** and **`PSPThumbnailMaker.exe`** from the
[**Releases**](https://github.com/SancioPanza88/psp-flix/releases) page.

Place `ffmpeg.exe` and `ffprobe.exe` next to them (or have ffmpeg on your PATH),
then run **PSP Flix**, search a film or TV series, download it and copy the
`.mp4` into the `VIDEO` folder of your PSP Memory Stick.

## Italiano

Scarica **`PSP Flix.exe`** e **`PSPThumbnailMaker.exe`** dalla pagina
[**Releases**](https://github.com/SancioPanza88/psp-flix/releases).

Metti `ffmpeg.exe` e `ffprobe.exe` nella stessa cartella (oppure installa ffmpeg
nel PATH), poi avvia **PSP Flix**, cerca un film o una serie, scaricalo e copia
il file `.mp4` nella cartella `VIDEO` della Memory Stick della PSP.
